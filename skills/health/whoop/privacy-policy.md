# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This is a personal-use app that connects my own WHOOP account to my personal AI assistant.

*Data collected:* Read-only WHOOP metrics I authorize — recovery, sleep, strain, workouts, and profile data. Nothing is written to WHOOP.

*How it's used:* Only to show me my own metrics and support my personal health tracking.

*Sharing:* Data is never sold or shared with third parties. OAuth tokens are stored securely by the assistant platform and never exposed.

*Revoking access:* I can revoke access at any time from the WHOOP app settings or Developer Dashboard, which immediately stops all data access.
