# Training Plan

Health goal: cut from 83 kg to 76 kg, build muscle, train consistently.
(Goal set 2026-10-05.)

## Mon — Push
**Areas:** chest, front and side delts, triceps, core

- Barbell or dumbbell bench press — 3 × 10–12
- Incline dumbbell press — 3 × 10–12
- Seated dumbbell shoulder press — 3 × 10–12
- Lateral raises — 3 × 12–15
- Tricep rope pushdown — 3 × 12–15
- Core block (5 min): plank 3 × 30–45 sec, dead bugs 3 × 10 per side

## Tue — Legs
**Areas:** quads, hamstrings, glutes, calves

- Goblet or back squat — 3 × 10–12
- Romanian deadlift — 3 × 10–12
- Walking lunges or split squats — 3 × 10 per leg
- Leg curl — 3 × 12–15
- Standing calf raises — 3 × 15–20

## Wed — Run
3 miles easy pace. Finish with 10 minutes of stretching for hips, hamstrings, and calves.

## Thu — Pull
**Areas:** lats, upper and mid back, lower back, rear delts, biceps, forearms, core

- Lat pulldown or assisted pull-ups — 3 × 10–12
- Seated cable row — 3 × 10–12
- Face pulls — 3 × 15 (rear delts and rotator cuff)
- Back extensions — 3 × 12–15
- Dumbbell curls — 3 × 10–12
- Farmer's carry — 3 × 30–40 sec (grip and forearms)
- Core block (5–10 min): hanging knee raises 3 × 10–15, Pallof press 3 × 12 per side

## Fri — Run
Intervals. 1 mile warm-up, then 4–6 rounds of 2–3 min hard with 2 min easy jog between, then 5–10 min cool-down.

## Sat — Rest
Full rest, or a 20–30 minute walk and light stretching if you want to move.

## Sun — Run + core
5 miles at an easy, conversational pace. Then a 5 min core block: side plank 3 × 30 sec per side, crunches 3 × 15.

---

## How to lift
- **Reps and sets:** 3 sets of 10–15 per exercise. The last 2 reps should be hard but your form stays clean.
- **Rest:** 60–90 seconds between sets. Shorter rest keeps conditioning up, which fits your endurance goal.
- **Warm-up:** 5 min of light cardio, then 1–2 light sets of your first exercise before working sets.
- **Progression:** When you hit 15 reps on all 3 sets, add a small amount of weight and drop back to 10 reps.
- **Form first.** At under a year of lifting, technique on squats, deadlifts, and presses matters more than the weight on the bar. Film a set occasionally to check yourself.

## How to run
- **Easy runs (Wed, Sun):** conversational pace. Most of your weekly mileage should be here. This is what actually builds endurance.
- **Intervals (Fri):** the hard efforts should feel genuinely hard, around an 8 out of 10. Don't let the easy jog between rounds become a hard one.
- **Building up:** increase total weekly mileage by no more than about 10% per week.
- **Deload:** every 4th week, cut mileage by roughly 25% to let your body absorb the work.
- **Shoes:** replace running shoes every 300–500 miles. Worn-out shoes are a common source of knee and shin problems.

## Recovery
- **Sleep:** 7–9 hours. Most of your adaptation happens here, not in the gym.
- **Protein:** spread it through the day, and eat something with protein and carbs within a couple hours of training.
- **Hydration:** matters more than most people think on run days.
- **Stretch or foam roll:** 10 min after runs, focused on hips, hamstrings, calves, and quads.
- **Listen to fatigue.** If you're run down, swap Friday's intervals for an easy run or take the day off. Persistent joint pain, unlike normal muscle soreness, means back off and get it checked.
