# Study Prep Space

A Muse space for studying and preparing technically — for interviews and for personal growth, in one place.

## What it is

A living app (in the Muse sidebar / Library) with a daily plan built around topics:

- **Focus Queue** — topics lined up in order; the top one is highlighted as "Up next"
- **Topic tags** — Interview, Personal, or Both
- **Topic status** — Queued → Studying → Confident, with reorder controls, a target date, notes, and saved resource links
- **Session logger** — records minutes studied per topic
- **This week strip** — minutes against an editable weekly goal, plus streak and all-time totals

It starts empty, with a few common starter topics to tap and add.

## Note

The space itself lives in Muse, and study progress is stored in the app — not in this repo. This folder is the record of what the space is and how it is organized.
